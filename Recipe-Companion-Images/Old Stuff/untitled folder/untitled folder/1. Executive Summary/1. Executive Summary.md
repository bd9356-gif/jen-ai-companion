1. Executive Summary  
  
MyAI Companion is a suite of AI-powered mobile apps spanning golf performance, recipe discovery, and cancer caregiver support — all conceived and directed by Bill, a 73-year-old entrepreneur who leveraged AI tools to bring his vision to life without writing a single line of code. This document captures the full marketing and PR strategy, including the core narrative, the multi-app suite positioning, timing considerations, and the contrast between earned media and paid promotion channels.  
  
“A 73-year-old founder used AI to build an entire app empire — and he’s just getting started.”  
  
That’s the headline we’re engineering.

2. The Core Story: AI Empowerment Angle  
  
The single most powerful marketing asset for MyAI Companion is not a feature list or a pricing model — it’s the founder story. The narrative of a 73-year-old non-technical entrepreneur harnessing AI to build a multi-app product suite cuts across every demographic and media vertical.  
  
Why This Story Works  
  
• Age-defying innovation: Contradicts the stereotype that tech entrepreneurship belongs to twenty-somethings in Silicon Valley. This resonates with aging-population media, AARP audiences, and general-interest outlets looking for uplifting stories.  
• AI as the great equalizer: Demonstrates that AI tools have lowered the barrier to entry for app development. You no longer need a CS degree or a dev team — you need a clear vision and persistence.  
• Relatable authenticity: Bill isn’t a tech mogul. He’s a real person solving real problems — better golf, easier cooking, cancer support for families. That relatability is media gold.  
• Multi-vertical appeal: The story pitches differently to tech press (AI democratization), lifestyle media (golf, recipes), health
